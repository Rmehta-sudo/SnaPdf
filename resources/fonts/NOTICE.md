# Bundled Noto fallback fonts

`NotoSans-Regular.ttf`, `NotoSansSymbols-Regular.ttf`, and
`NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf` are from the Noto font project, Copyright
Google LLC and contributors. They are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1. The license and source are available from the
[Noto Fonts project](https://github.com/notofonts/notofonts.github.io).

SnaPdf includes them only as text-rendering fallbacks so the portable AppImage
can show common Unicode, scientific, and technical symbols consistently.
